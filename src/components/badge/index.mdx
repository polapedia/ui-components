## Colors & Variants

Badges support multiple colors and emphasis levels:

- **Colors**: `red`, `neutral`, `danger`, `warning`, `success`, `info`.
- **Variants**:
  - `primary`: Solid/Gradient background with white or dark text.
  - `secondary`: Subtle background with colored text.
  - `tertiary`: Outlined with colored text.
